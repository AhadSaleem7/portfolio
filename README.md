# portfolio
portfolio of Khushi developed by Ahad Saleem as a project .
Overview
The portfolio website showcases the work and skills of Khushi, a passionate graphic designer. The site is designed to highlight various projects, services, and contact information. It serves as a digital playground where visitors can explore Khushi’s creative work and professional background.

Usage Instructions
Home Page: This is the landing page where you get a brief introduction to Khushi and her work.
About Page: Provides detailed information about Khushi’s background, skills, and professional journey.
Services Page: Lists the services offered, such as graphic design, web development, and more.
Projects Page: Showcases a portfolio of completed projects, each with a description and visual representation.
Contact Page: Contains a form for visitors to get in touch with Khushi for inquiries or collaborations.
Resume Download: Offers an option to download Khushi’s resume for a more detailed look at her qualifications and experience.
